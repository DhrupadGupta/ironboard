How it flows

From walk-in
to renewal.

A single member journey, handed cleanly from one department's board to the next.

Reception

Prospect walks in, trials a session, and reception registers them with a Member ID.

Membership

A plan is chosen and activated, with renewal reminders queued automatically.

Trainer

A workout plan is assigned and progress is logged against the member's profile.

Accounting

Payments are collected, invoiced, and reconciled against membership status.

Admin

Attendance, staff and equipment are monitored across every branch, always.
